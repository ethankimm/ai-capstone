**Interpretation:**
- "Chosen constants are combined near the end" is wrong in all five checkpoints. "A rough value picks branches" overstated it: the value is close to exact with a margin of 2 or more through rule 4, and resolution degrades along the chain.
- Reach comes from rule-to-rule passing packed into layers about 8-11, at under about one layer per hop by this blunt measure. Whether the layer budget sets the L5-7 fall-off is suggested, not shown.
- The value looks factored: magnitude and low bit survive where exact value does not. Halving breaks the low bit, as expected, because the next bit up becomes the low bit. Consistent with Li, Guo and Andreas (2503.02854) on parity-first state tracking; not a test of their mechanism.
- The null at L5-7 has two parts. Most natural items do not need the precision CODI adds, and items that do are near the floor for both. Task slack dilutes the gain; it does not hide a deep-chain gain, because latents read a late state and cannot repair earlier decisions.

**Caveats:** One seed per recipe. Constructed chains are zero-shot and rare in training, so absolute accuracy may understate a model trained on them. The margin cut (<= 2) and tight-count split were chosen after seeing data; the gain replicates across two recipes but was not pre-registered. Single-block gains are windows between arrival and last read, not arrival times, and overlap across layers. Probes are linear and the second bit stays near chance, which may be a probe limit.

**Reading:** with used latents, the added precision is at the last rule only. That is one extra step at the end, worth about a fifth of a level of reach, not one level and not six. Rule count does measure serial steps, since the state passes rule by rule, so "reach is not serial depth" in 20261105-085236 was wrong. A tight threshold is a probe of the state at one rule, not a replacement for depth. The position split was chosen after seeing the data, though the earlier v_{n-1} result points the same way.
