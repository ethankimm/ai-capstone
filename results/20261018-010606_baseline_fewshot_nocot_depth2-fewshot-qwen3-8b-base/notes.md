**Goal:** For depth2 (rule chain), does the number of rules a pretrained model can chain without CoT grow with its layer count? One model of the depth-vs-layers scan (Pythia, Qwen2.5, Qwen3, Ouro loops).

**Data:** synthetic-depth2 test (generator 2.0.0), n=2400; run seed=0.
**Hyperparams:** {'n_shots': 6, 'shot_seed': 0, 'shot_source': 'train, same level', 'per_level': 200, 'cot_per_level': 50, 'max_new': 6, 'cot_max_new': 200, 'reach_floor': 0.08, 'loops': None, 'cot_loops': None, 'decoding': 'greedy', 'backend': 'vllm'}

**Interpretation:** Scan design (shared by all 20 runs of this scan, 2026-10-17/18): pretrained base models, no fine-tuning; 6-shot `Question: ...\nAnswer: N` prompts with shots drawn from depth2 train at the same level as the query (shot_seed 0); greedy, at most 6 new tokens, stop at newline; first 200 test items per level (levels 1-12; Ouro: levels 1-4 only, see gotchas). CoT reference: same shots with the applied arithmetic written out (`83-18=65. The answer is 65.`), first 50 items per level (Ouro: 20). Reach = largest L such that every level <= L has Wilson 95% lower bound above 0.08 (about 2x the shortcut floors: majority answer 0.03-0.04, first-/last-rule-only <= 0.05 for L >= 2). Summary table and plot: `scripts/depth_scaling_summary.py`. Across the scan: no model has reach above 1. Accuracy at 1 rule grows with scale (Qwen2.5 0.19 at 0.5B to 0.97 at 32B; Qwen3 0.14 to 0.92), but accuracy at 2 rules stays at or near floor in every model (max 0.10, Qwen2.5-32B, 64 layers), while the fine-tuned 12-layer GPT-2 no-CoT model gets 0.77 at 2 rules and 0.49 at 3 (`20261014-021704_filler_tokens_v2-depth2-nocot-25ep`). Same-depth pairs: Qwen3-0.6B vs 1.7B (both 28 layers) 0.14 vs 0.28 at L1; Qwen3-4B vs 8B (both 36) 0.84 vs 0.84; Qwen2.5-7B (28 layers) beats Qwen2.5-3B (36 layers) at L1, 0.61 vs 0.39. Ouro-1.4B at fixed weights: loops 1-4 (24-96 effective layers) lift L1 0.16 -> 0.51 but leave L2 at floor (0.01-0.04); Ouro-2.6B, loops 1-4 (48-192 effective layers): L1 0.25 -> 0.90, L2 0.03-0.04, while its CoT reference is 0.90 at L2 and 0.65 at L3. At L2 the larger Qwen2.5 models mostly answer with a one-rule result (14B/32B: ~22% the rule-1 intermediate, ~20% rule 2 applied to the start). Reading: in-context, without CoT, these models do about one rule per forward pass regardless of layer count (24-64 layers, or up to 192 effective for Ouro), so the answer to 'does depth reach track layers' is no in this regime: the limit is not layer count but what the pretrained circuits do in one pass; fine-tuning a 12-layer model gets past it.

**Caveats:** One prompt format, one shot seed, no prompt search; base models only (no instruct). 200 items per level (95% CI about +-7 pt at 0.5). Layer count, width, params and training data co-vary within each family; the same-depth pairs and Ouro loops are the only controls, and they are few. Few-shot no-CoT is a lower bound on what the weights can do (the fine-tuned GPT-2 shows the task is learnable at 12 layers). Pythia <= 1.4B cannot execute one rule even with CoT (CoT L1 <= 0.20), so its no-CoT curve says nothing about depth. CoT reference n=50 per level.
