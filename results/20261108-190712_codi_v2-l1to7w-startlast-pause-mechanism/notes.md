**Interpretation:** At L3 the positions compute and store the value before the last rule more exactly than CODI's latents, so they are not idle, and their gain over CODI-0 at L3 (+.258) is the same as CODI's. But the answer does not depend on them the way start-last's answer depends on its latents: hiding all of them keeps 87% (60% at L3), and a donor's keys/values scramble the answer instead of moving it (the recipient's question tokens still carry the old value, so two sources conflict). There is no relay between positions (masking the answer from a patched position leaves nothing, as in start-last) and no extra reliance on the look-back (cut costs pause as little as or less than CODI, the opposite of the prediction in section 15.3). Vote-by-count is present but weak. Reading: the pause positions are extra parallel width at one hop, read jointly with the question tokens; they are not a horizontal chain. Start-last's recurrent input is what makes its latents the sole route to the answer and what carries the gain to L4-L6 (its hide-all collapses to .06 and its pass 1 is essential). This is a mechanism reading from one seed.
