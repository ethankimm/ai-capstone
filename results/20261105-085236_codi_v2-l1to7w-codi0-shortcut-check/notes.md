## 2026-11-05 — Is CODI-0's reach a shortcut? No: no-tracking solvers fall far short and single-rule edits show it uses every rule, but reach (d50) is not serial depth, and its lead over no_thoughts is still unexplained (codi, run_id: 20261105-085236_codi_v2-l1to7w-codi0-shortcut-check)

**Goal:** CODI-0 reaches ~5 rules in one pass against ~2 for `no_thoughts`. Is that a shortcut? Follow-up to 20261105-074801, whose "tracks the path" reading assumed closeness needs the chain.
**Mechanism / model:** `codi`, gpt2. Stabilised CODI-0 and CODI, lr-3e-3 L1-7w CODI, round-1 CODI-0. fp32, MPS.
**Data:** 140 canonical test items per level, L4-7 (560), plus 4,659 edited variants (seed 5). Solvers: all 3,500 L1-7 test items, priors from train.json. Edited items are new questions, so no test statistic is reused for selection.

**Interpretation:** No shortcut of the kinds tested: CODI-0 reads every rule, picks branches hard and carries constants at gain ~1, and information-limited solvers cannot reach its accuracy. But "single-pass serial depth" overstates what d50 measures. Exact s_j at rule tokens is weak (0.51 at rule 2, 0.27 at rule 4, 20261103-063912) while exact answers are not (0.76 at L4), and branch probes climb into layers 9-11 for rules 3-6. This suggests two tracks: a coarse running value sets branches (the serial part, tolerant of error), and the selected constants are combined near the end. That is inferred, not shown, and would also explain near-miss errors. The gap to `no_thoughts` is not an architecture limit: same model, L2 0.41 vs 0.99. Budget differs a lot (no-CoT 9.4k steps at lr 1e-4, no_thoughts ~2.3k in its final format, CODI-0 ~30k at lr 3e-3 LoRA) as do the losses, and these are not separated.
**Gotchas hit:** Near-misses also appear in T5 (20261019-034448) and plain no-CoT, so they do not come from distillation.
**Caveats:** One seed per model. Conditioning on a close unedited answer removes items the model already fails. Exact-s_j numbers are linear probes at one token, so the two-track reading could be a probe limit. Early-position cells are small (n = 14-42). The prior solver uses kind and threshold only, not a learned surface model; the Bayes solvers bound the information in the rules, not every possible heuristic.
