**Interpretation:** This makes F5 a complete ground-truth table. A linear probe reading a value at ~1.00 says nothing about whether the model uses that latent: 13 of the 48 gated and SIM cells are readable and unused, with use measured by swaps rather than inferred. A latent monitor built on probes would report a faithful trace at positions the answer ignores (Lanham-style unfaithfulness, the "decorative" kind). The untrained logit lens has the opposite failure. It is faithful in gated, where values sit in number-token directions, and blind to most used SIM latents, where they do not. So no single decoder is faithful across methods without a causal check. Plain CODI is the third case: use is spread over near-copies, and no decoder reads the value from any one of them. A per-latent table there would call every latent "neither" although the model depends on the set. Group-level tests (b29) are the right unit for CODI.
