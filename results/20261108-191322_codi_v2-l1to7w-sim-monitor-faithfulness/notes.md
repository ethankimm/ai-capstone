## 2026-11-08 — Monitor test on SIM-CODI start-first: on-route monitors stay faithful after swaps (entries .91-1.00, falling to .71-.82 at the deepest sites of L6-L7); first own-error test on a forced model: the best single-latent monitor predicts .25 of the model's own wrong answers (6/24) against .01-.02 by chance (codi, run_id: 20261108-191322_codi_v2-l1to7w-sim-monitor-faithfulness)

**Goal:** t21b (pre-registered, t_predictions.md t21, commit 96a04d4): steps 2-3 of the latent-monitor check (decode at the causal location, then test faithfulness under swaps and against the model's own errors) on SIM-CODI start-first. This is the first forced model with errors at all; gated and start-last SIM had none on valid L3-L5 (20261108-102225), so their own-error test was empty.
**Mechanism / model:** SIM-CODI start-first checkpoint-17820 (20261108-130905), loaded without the step decoder.
**Data:** decoders fit on 1200 train questions per level, L2-L7 (7,200; t19 used 1200 per level, L2-L6). Tested on valid L3-L7 with t21a's items and full-swap donors (20261108-190213 route split). On-route (t21a input only >= .5): L3 {0,1}, L4 {0,1,2}, L5 {0..3}, L6 {0..4}, L7 {0..4}. 24 wrong items at L5-L7 (5, 7, 12), none at L3-L4.
**Command:** `run_guarded.sh t21b.log scripts/necessity/rc_monitor.py simsf mps t21b_simsf.json 1200` (log in `scripts/codi_null_audit/prereg/`); record via `prereg/record21.py t21b`. A first launch with the default 600 per level was stopped before decoders were trained, to match t19.
**Headline results** (monitor answer = decoded value pushed through the remaining rules):
- **Clean decoding, on-route sites:** entries .88-1.00; input .97 at z_0, then .77-.84 at z_1 and .57-.71 deeper (start-last SIM .84-.99 on both).
- **After a swap at an on-route site, monitor answer = actual answer:** entries .91-1.00 at L3-L5 and at shallower sites of L6-L7, .82 (L6 z_4) and .71 / .84 (L7 z_4 / z_3); input .95-.98 at z_0, falling to .49-.77 deeper.
- **Off-route sites (L3-L4 spares):** input monitors decorative (.01-.20); entries monitors .17-.73 (the swapped input barely changes those entries, as in t19).
- **Own errors, pooled L5-L7 (n = 24):** best single site .25 (input z_3; entries z_4 .25), other sites .00-.21; a random value through the same rules hits the wrong answer .01-.02. Union over sites (any latent's read explains the error) was .69 (9/13) in the earlier post-hoc s2 analysis of L3-L6 (20261108-134158).

**Interpretation:** The on-route monitors are faithful: change a trained latent and the decoded trace predicts the new answer, as on start-last. They are less precise than start-last's, and the loss grows with depth (input decoders .97 at z_0 to .57 deep; entries monitors .71-.82 at the deepest L6-L7 sites), matching the noisier latent code seen in the probes (.52 vs .82). The own-error test, impossible on the other forced models, gives a positive but partial result: single-latent monitors predict a quarter of the errors, about 10x chance, so errors are partly visible in the trace as a wrong carried value. Pre-registered thresholds: swap agreement >= .85 met by entries monitors except the deepest sites of L6-L7, missed by input monitors past z_1; own errors >= .3 at the best on-route site missed (.25, n = 24). Caution from t19: relay CODI's non-causal decoders predicted .36-.50 of its own errors, so own-error prediction certifies a monitor only together with the swap test, which passes here.
**Gotchas hit:** Own-error n is small (24); the per-level rates (.14-.40) rest on 5-12 items each.
**Caveats:** One seed, valid only, linear decoders. Swap n = recipients answered right with a valid donor (273-285 per level).
