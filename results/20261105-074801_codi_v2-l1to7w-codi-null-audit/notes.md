## 2026-11-04 — Audit of the CODI = CODI-0 null on RuleChain: no bug; stabilised latents inert until epoch 9; used latents hold end-relative v_{n-1} and stop, a gain of about one hop (round 1 L2-3 23 vs 1), while L5-7 errors are mostly near-misses (codi, run_id: 20261105-074801_codi_v2-l1to7w-codi-null-audit)

**Goal:** Find out why CODI does not beat CODI-0 on RuleChain, when it does on GSM8K (+0.074, `origin/kevin/lrm-training-2`). Full write-up: `CODI_NULL_AUDIT.md`.
**Mechanism / model:** `codi`, gpt2. Stabilised pair (20261105-044444 / 20261105-024111, all 12 epochs), lr-3e-3 L1-7w CODI (20261103-193425), round-1 pair, released `zen-E/CODI-gpt2`. fp32, CPU and MPS.
**Data:** Canonical L1-L7 test predictions (already logged), valid for the time course, train samples for probes, GSM8K test for the released model. No new test eval of the pair; `predictions.jsonl` is a per-item analysis table of the logged answers.
**Command:** `scripts/codi_null_audit/` (see its README); `b*` need the CODI venv.

**Interpretation:** No bug. The stabilised null barely tests latents: they do nothing for two-thirds of training. Where latents are used they do not step through the chain: they converge on the value the distillation target holds (v_{n-1}) and stay there, which is why they look like copies, and they buy about one hop. That hop pays on GSM8K at 1-3 steps and on RuleChain at L2-3, where CODI removes most of CODI-0's errors but CODI-0 is already at 0.96-0.99. At L5-7 the latent would itself have to get a 4-6-rule value exact in about one pass, and the remaining errors are mostly exactness, not path: CODI-0 is about as close as SIM-Coconut at L5-6 and differs in exact-given-close (0.53-0.69 vs 0.97-0.98).
**Corrections to earlier records:** (1) "Partial, early-rule summary" in 20261104-034628, 20261105-053042 and 20261104-070914 is largely a pooled-level artefact: those probes are forward-indexed, and v_2 is v_{n-1} at L3. (2) "The recurrence reaches a fixed point" should not be read as contraction. (3) `RULECHAIN_RESULTS.md`'s "matches CODI-0 at every level" misses the paired L2-3 gain.
**Gotchas hit:** My harness took swap to 49 GB (LM-head logits on question tokens, uncapped MPS allocator); stopped, fixed, and later jobs ran serially under a swap guard. Setting only the high MPS watermark fails until the low one is set too. Released GSM8K CODI has numbers on even latents, Kevin's on odd ones: a first lens pass on odd sites gave all zeros. Wrong ideas tested and dropped: a constant-noise tracker simulator failed its held-out checks (curve shape, item correlation ~ -0.05, halving backwards) though doubling does shift the error spectrum (CODI |e|=4 0.035 -> 0.160); "the teacher ':' is just the answer"; "CODI-0 benefits by holding v_{n-1} at the readout" (it holds it at 0.013).
**Caveats:** One seed per model. The link from distillation target to latent content is inferred, not intervened on. Linear probes are lower bounds. Gradient shares use 30-item samples (three at epochs 8-12, one elsewhere). SIM-Coconut differs in stack and data (25k items, full fine-tuning, curriculum), so that comparison is descriptive. The GSM8K lens is the released model, not Kevin's 100k one. Non-exact metrics are exploratory: the within +-2 pair gain (+0.024, p 0.035, uncorrected) shrinks to +0.007 on valid and is treated as noise. The L2-3 test was predicted from this audit's data, and is not pre-registered. How CODI-0 reaches its answer is unresolved: the answer appears only at layers 11-12 of ':'.
