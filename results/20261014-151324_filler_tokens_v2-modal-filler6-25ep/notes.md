**Caveats:** **Confound:** "faithful" filler (cot_rate 0.5, unmasked filler loss) is trained with CoT supervision on half the examples and with the filler tokens positions in the loss, while the no-CoT baseline never sees a CoT. So +7.9 pt is not attributable to extra filler compute alone; it may come from the CoT supervision. A plain (non-faithful) filler-6 run and a random-token filler control are needed to isolate the filler effect.
